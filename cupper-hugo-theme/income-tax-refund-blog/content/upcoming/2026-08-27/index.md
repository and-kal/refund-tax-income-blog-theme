==Income U-Bac Refund presents==

**LUGE** (_funprog_ / Canada)

We're stoked as a colony of ants in a soda can to have Toronto's LUGE coming to town for the premier time. Miscount your blessings and dip into this vortex of dad-metal guitar solos (played in the funky manner). Get thrashed by those fuzzy grooves. Take brief chances to get some footwork going in the groovy bits; and at the same time: good luck making sense of those mindaltering lyrics.

**Iz Kasper** (_triphop_ / Lpz)

A further chance to trip out a little will be offered by Leipzig's _Iz Kasper_ trio, who will be elevating some keys-based bedroom dream pop songs to murky climes of muddy basses, with situative percussions dropping in and out, and melodies and voices coating you in sweet glue.
