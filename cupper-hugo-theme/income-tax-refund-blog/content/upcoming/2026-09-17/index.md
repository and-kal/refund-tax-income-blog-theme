[Geo](https://geoband.bandcamp.com/) are a classic band outfit from the Netherlands, dwelling somewhere deep inside the post-punk adjustment where things are tight, twitchy, and just a little bit wrong. Their sound carries a no-wave attitude, bouncy rhythms, and nerve-rattling tension, all wrapped up in a kind of disorder with a catchy little smile.

─────────

[GRUMPER](https://grumperrr.bandcamp.com/album/demo) are a noise-rock outfit from Brno, Czech Republic, operating somewhere between art punk and total sensory overload. Fast, harsh and restless, they turn nervous energy into a minor electrical emergency.
