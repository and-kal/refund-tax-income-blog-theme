**Kaskader** (_jagged punk_, FR/GR/PL)

> Kaskader was hired by an international association to test the resistance of rock in opposition and post punk structures to compositional mania.

Following the marvelous microtonal jams of Strzał w Kolano and Tsev on their [tour in May](/past/2026-05-14), the two expanded into a 4-piece that will lay down some symphonies of teasing rhythm changes and jerky melodies.

**Pacific Interest** (_bubble ambient_ / Essen)

...is the new solo project of Janik from Portugal's chillpsychslackers [Just Fish](https://justfish.bandcamp.com/) by means of which he will draw the listeners into some eerie aquatic sound scenarios and make one move like seaweeds or plankton.
